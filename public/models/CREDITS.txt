Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made for Kempen 222: made smaller for the web (textures reduced to 1024 px WebP, mesh simplified and

ballot-box.glb
--------------
A low-poly Malaysian ballot box (clear plastic body, slotted lid, label and seal strap), supplied by the project's owner for Kempen 222.
It is used as it was supplied; the ballots that fill it are made in code (src/ui/stage/BallotBox.tsx).

voting-booth.glb
----------------
A low-poly polling table with a three-sided cardboard screen, a pencil on a string, a ballot paper and a stamp, supplied by the project's
owner for Kempen 222. It is used as it was supplied, except that the stamp takes the party's colour and moves in code
(src/ui/stage/VotingBooth.tsx).
