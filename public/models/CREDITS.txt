politician.glb
--------------
"Indian Man in suit" by Pixel_Monster (https://sketchfab.com/ar.jethin)
Source:  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/indian-man-in-suit-985dd9756d89464b9380414b5b12d8aa

Changes made for Kempen 222: made smaller for the web (textures reduced to 1024 px WebP, mesh simplified and
compressed with meshopt, teeth and the single-frame animation removed). It is posed and animated in code
(src/ui/stage/Politician.tsx). The author and licence are also kept inside the file.

ballot-box.glb
--------------
A low-poly Malaysian ballot box (clear plastic body, slotted lid, label and seal strap), supplied by the project's owner for Kempen 222.
It is used as it was supplied; the ballots that fill it are made in code (src/ui/stage/BallotBox.tsx).
